Triad cards show which triad the line comes from, and what that triad is over the chord under it. Grey cards follow a held note while the chord changes under it; the small numbers along a long note show its job at each chord.

The runs in bars 1, 13 and 17 are two triads interleaved, so their cards sit in two lanes. That's the triad-pair idea from the 2–5 app, used by the melody itself.
