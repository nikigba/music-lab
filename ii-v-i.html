ii–V ear lessons

One Dm7 triad, two or three ways to the dominant. Listen first, then guess, then sing where the top voice goes.

LickNumbers in the circles are measured from C; small numbers above are the note over its chord. Tap a note to hear it; tap a grey card to reveal the shape hiding there.

Tap any card to hear just that triad. The Dm7 triad rolls up; the G7 triad starts on its top note, one step from where the Dm7 triad ended, then plays its other two notes. With Mix on, those two come in a random order each time, and the numbers on the card follow what you heard. Colors on the dominant options show how hard they pull toward C: peach pulls a little, orange more, red the most. The dots count notes that sit a half step from C, E or G. The brackets after each name show its three notes as tones of its chord (Dm7 or G7); the one sounding lights up. Numbers are always measured from C.
