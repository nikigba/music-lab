Triads by Chord

Which triads the players in your library use over major, minor and dominant chords. Numbers are counted from the chord root, not the key. Tap a triad to hear it and play real licks, all moved onto one chord root. Tap ☆ on a lick to save it to your ★ Favorite licks.

Reading every solo in the library…

How it counts: three notes in a row inside one phrase, all over the same chord, that make a major, minor, diminished or augmented triad within an octave. Going up and down the same triad counts once. Sus, half-diminished and diminished chords are left out. Gold numbers are the triad, grey numbers are the notes around it, all as degrees of the chord. Weimar solos play with their recorded rhythm, so swing is only added to the others.
