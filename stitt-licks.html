Reading the lick

Half step (shown once all are revealed) Passing note, outside the shape The two guide-tone voices (3rds and 7ths) Gold is home (C); the farther a shape sits from C, the cooler its color

The big number is the note's distance from C. The small number under it shows up when you reveal its card: it's the note's place in that shape. In lick 1, A♭ is ♭6 from C but the ♭3 of F°. Long circles are long notes.

Voice leading. The two colored rows under the chord names are the guide-tone voices, in numbers from C: in a ii–V–I that's 1 → 7 → 7 (♭7 → 3 → 7) and 4 → 4 → 3 (♭3 → ♭7 → 3). At each change only one voice moves, by a half step (the pill shows it); the faded number with a dash is a voice that holds. Under the rows, each change says what the lick does there: a ✓ means the line steps right onto the moving voice; otherwise it says where it lands instead. Lick notes that sit on a guide tone get a small colored dot, and a lick step that follows the moving voice is drawn thick in that voice's color. Dots under the lick numbers count how many changes follow a voice: start with those.

Hear picks what plays: the lick, the lick with the two voices held quietly underneath, or the voices alone with the bass. Tap a row's button (▶ 1 → 7 → 7) to hear one voice by itself. Then sing it over the lick.

Watch the half steps at the chord changes. The 7th of one chord falling to the 3rd of the next (C → B, F → E) is the guide-tone line, and most of these licks are built around it. When a lick skips it, the card says what replaces it.

Not every card is a triad. Some bars are scale runs or neighbor figures, and several G7 bars use a piece of the altered scale (♭9 and ♯9) instead of a triad. In set 1, licks 1–8 use the ♭9 on G7; from lick 9 on it's the natural 9. Colors follow the Triad Palette; diminished shapes are greyed.

Set 1 was analyzed by hand. Later sets are analyzed by script, so their card notes are plainer; licks the script couldn't read cleanly (odd chords, very long or very fast lines) are left out until checked. Long or fast licks scroll sideways.

Keys: Space plays or stops, 1–9 pick a lick, Esc stops. Tap a note first (it gets a gold ring) and Space plays from that note; Esc clears it. The Play button always starts from the top.
