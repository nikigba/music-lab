How the loop works
Global home: the chord, then 1-3-5. Your true north.
Home: a clean shape, like 3-1-5.
Departure: the same shape with one outside note added, like 3-1-♭5-5.
Return: the clean shape again, so you hear what was taken away.
Your turn: sing it or play it back in the gap. If you only listen, it turns into background music.
