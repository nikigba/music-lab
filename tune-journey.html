Tune Journey

A whole solo as a trip away from home. The faint sun in the middle is the key's home triad. The bright column is the chord of the moment: chords that belong to the key stay close, chords with notes from outside the key travel further away. When the soloist plays 1, 3 or 5 of home, the sun lights up, even while the chords are away. Notes glow around the chord, colored by how they sit on it. Every tune is shown in C.
