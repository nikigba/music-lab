Home & Away · ii–V–I

Home is the C triad, 1 3 5. Every other triad is somewhere away from it, and you can hear how far: the fewer home notes a triad keeps, the farther away it sounds. Notes from outside the key (spice) push it farther still. A ii–V–I is a trip down this ladder and back: the ii bar steps a little away, the V bar goes far, then home. All numbers are measured from C.

Home note (1 3 5) In the key, not home Spice: outside the key Away: home → farthest

The ladder: how far is each triad from home?

Tap a triad: you hear home → that triad → home, each on its own root, so the triad sounds like itself. Listen for how far it feels from home. Turn on Smooth voicing to keep the shared notes still and hear only the notes that move away. The drone stays off here unless you turn it on, because a C under everything makes every triad sound like a kind of C.

Tap a triad.

What to listen for

  1. Go down the ladder one rung at a time. Each rung keeps fewer home notes, and the tension grows.
  2. F and G keep one home note each (1 or 5): away, but you can still hear home. Dm and B° keep none.
  3. B° holds 7 and 4, the two notes that most want to go home. The spice triads (Fm, A♭, F°, D♭) add notes from outside the key: darker and farther.
  4. Sing the note that moves. Then close your eyes, tap a triad, and guess its rung.

Build a trip

Pick a triad for the ii bar (a small step away) and one for the V bar (far away). The app plays each triad as a line, in the shape you choose, and lands home. This is how the licks are built: choose how far to go in each bar, then draw the triad as a line. Each change also tells you which notes stay and which ones move.
