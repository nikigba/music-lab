Chromatic names

Your piano plays, the app shows each note as a number with its syllable underneath. In Practice, sing first and play second, one tiny step at a time. If your piano also makes its own sound, leave app sound off so you do not hear doubles. Tapping the on-screen keys always plays the app's piano.

·Press a key on your piano or tap one below. The gold dot is Do.
