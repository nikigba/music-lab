Solo ear lessons

Transcribed solos with every note hidden: hear a bar, work it out by ear, reveal to check. The other tabs take the techniques found in these solos and drill them through every key.

Drop MusicXML files here or tap to choose. They're added to the list.
Calm: settled in the chord and at home Strong: settled in the chord, pulling home Early: home already, rubs the chord Spice: settled in neither, passing

Tap a bar number to hear that bar, or ▶ on a row to hear four bars. Tap a ? to hear one note over its bass; tap it as often as you like to hear it again, and double-tap it quickly to reveal it. Space plays and pauses; after you tap a note, Space plays from that note. To loop a stretch: hold a note or bar number for a moment and slide over the rest, then let go. (Or tap Select, then the first and last; or Shift-click on a computer.) Big numbers are from the key centre you pick; small numbers above are the note over its chord. Orange links are half steps. Tap a grey card to hear the triad hiding there and see its name. Dashed circles are passing notes. The slim bars under the cards are pentatonic phrases: runs of six or more notes that all sit in one pentatonic. Tap one to hear its scale and see which it is. Runs, Approach and Motifs add more lanes: scale runs (and whether they use the key's scale, the chord's, or bebop passing notes), enclosures and half-step approaches into chord tones, and shapes that come back.
