Major triad trainer

Hear what a major triad does over a bass note, drill it in patterns through every key, then find it in your own licks and solos.

Every major triad over one C bass note. The numbers say what the three notes are over C; the word says what it sounds like. Tap a card to hear the bass, the triad rolled up, then all of it together.

Every major-triad cell found in your Stitt, Parker and Kellen ii–V licks and the Hargrove and Cherokee solos (both read in C). Numbers are degrees of the triad: 1 3 5 are triad notes, purple ones are approach or passing notes. ▶ plays it as it was played; 12 keys drills it around the cycle of 4ths, keeping the same relation to the chord.
